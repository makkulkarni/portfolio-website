Agricultural Risk

Crop insurance, yield estimation and pest/disease risk tools.

Stochastic Yield Shock Engine: 1,000+ Monte Carlo draws simulating shortfall payout distributions: $Y = \max\left(0, \; \mu + Z \cdot \sigma\right)$.
Contractual PMFBY Payouts: Computes shortfall payout ratio: $\min\left(1, \; \max\left(0, \; \frac{\text{TY} \times (1 - \text{indemnity}) - Y}{\text{TY}}\right)\right)$.
Hierarchical Portfolio Pooling: Sums liabilities simultaneously across $\text{IU} \to \text{District} \to \text{Cluster}$.
Actuarial Tail Risk (VaR): Computes Expected Burn Rates, inter-annual volatility ($\sigma$), and $P_{90}$ / $P_{95}$ Value at Risk.

Insurance & Reinsurance Analytics

Portfolio risk, loss modelling and pricing simulators.

PMFBY Master Dataset: Synthesizes 1,870+ scheme records across 77 districts with dynamic cluster builders.
Excess of Loss (XL) Layers: Structures non-proportional treaties ($C \text{ xs } D$) against aggregate loss draws: $Y = \min(\max(X - D, 0), C)$.
Burning Cost & Volatility Loadings: Prices layers with standard deviation loading: $\text{Layer Rate} = \mathbb{E}[Y] + \lambda \times \sigma(Y)$.
Rate on Line (ROL %): Continuous band scaling, attachment probabilities, and Exceedance Probability (EP) curves.
