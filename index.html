🌤️ Actuarial & Climate Volatility

PMFBY Crop Insurance Portfolio & Tail Risk Analyzer

▸ Stochastic Yield Shock Modeling: Generates 1,000+ Monte Carlo simulated yield trajectories per Insurance Unit (IU) using Gaussian shocks: $Y_{i,s} = \max(0, \mu_i + Z_s \cdot \sigma_i)$, with actuarial minimum variance buffers.
▸ Contractual Indemnity Mechanics: Applies contractual Threshold Yields ($TY$) and indemnity levels to evaluate shortfalls: $\text{Payout Ratio} = \min\left(1, \max\left(0, \frac{TY_i \times (1 - \text{indemnity}) - Y_{i,s}}{TY_i} ight) ight)$.
▸ Hierarchical Spatial Risk Pooling: Sums currency liabilities simultaneously from $\text{IU} \to \text{District} \to \text{Cluster}$, capturing the geographical diversification effect where low losses offset high claims.
▸ Tail Risk & Bidding Optimization: Calculates Expected Burn Rates, inter-annual volatility ($\sigma$), 1-in-10 ($P_{90}$) and 1-in-20 ($P_{95}$) Value at Risk scenarios, and optimal cluster pairings.

⚡ Reinsurance Capital & Pricing

Non-Proportional XL Reinsurance Pricing & Risk Simulator

▸ Excess of Loss (XL) Treaty Modeling: Actuarial pricing platform structuring non-proportional layers ($C \text{ xs } D$) against annual aggregate loss trajectories: $Y = \min(\max(X - D, 0), C)$.
▸ Multi-Distribution Fitting: Executes 10,000+ stochastic draws across Normal, heavy-tailed Lognormal, and skewed Gamma distributions fitted via method of moments.
▸ Continuous Band Scaling: Auto-constructs continuous coverage bands from zero to upper treaty boundaries, scaling layer deductibles and limits via target loss ratios ($LR$).
▸ Rate on Line (ROL %) & Exceedance: Derives pure burning cost plus standard deviation volatility loadings ($\text{Layer Rate} = \mathbb{E}[Y] + \lambda \times \sigma(Y)$), Rate on Line (ROL %), relative layer shares, and Exceedance Probability (EP) curves.
