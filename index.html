PMFBY Crop Insurance Portfolio & Tail Risk Analyzer

▸ Stochastic Yield Shock Modeling: Generates 1,000+ Monte Carlo simulated yield trajectories per Insurance Unit (IU) based on historical empirical distributions and actuarial minimum variance buffers.

▸ Hierarchical Portfolio Pooling: Sums currency liabilities simultaneously from IU $ o$ District $ o$ Cluster, capturing the geographical diversification effect where low losses offset high claims.
▸ Tail Risk & Bidding Optimization: Calculates Expected Burn Rates, inter-annual volatility (SD), 1-in-10 ($P_{90}$) and 1-in-20 ($P_{95}$) Value at Risk scenarios, and optimal cluster pairings.

📈 Underwriting Intelligence

PMFBY Underwriting Analytics Platform & Cluster Builder

⚡ Reinsurance Capital & Pricing

Non-Proportional XL Reinsurance Pricing & Risk Simulator

▸ Excess of Loss (XL) Treaty Modeling: Actuarial pricing platform that structures non-proportional layers ($C ext{ xs } D$) against annual aggregate loss trajectories.
▸ Multi-Distribution Fitting: Executes 10,000+ stochastic draws across Normal, heavy-tailed Lognormal, and skewed Gamma distributions fitted via method of moments.
▸ Continuous Band Scaling: Auto-constructs continuous coverage bands from zero to upper treaty boundaries, scaling layer deductibles and limits via target loss ratios ($LR\%$).
▸ Rate on Line (ROL %) & Exceedance: Derives pure burning cost plus standard deviation volatility loadings ($ar{Y} + \lambda \cdot \sigma$), ROL %, relative layer shares, and Exceedance Probability (EP) curves.
